    Shackleton
    Station

    Step onto the Moon and run a real-feeling base for 6 days. Walk around, drive the rover, collect rocks and decide where the power goes. Keep your crew breathing, fed and safe, and run for cover when the Sun flares. The flares are real ones recorded by NASA.

    Checking today's real space weather with NASA…
